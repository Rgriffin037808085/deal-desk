How to update this page
  • Claude version (this page on claude.ai): press Update listings. It also refreshes by itself every evening.
  • Password page (GitHub link): it does not refresh by itself. In the Business Finder project, ask Claude to "republish the standalone page" and give your password, then reload the page.
  • Change what you search for: use "What I'm looking for" on the claude.ai version, then press Update listings.
  • Notes and stages: saved in your account on claude.ai, and only in that browser on the password page.
  • The full guide is deal-desk-how-to.md in the project's files.
